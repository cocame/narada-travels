Tour to Ba Na Hills and the Golden Bridge from Da Nang

Tour from Da Nang

Two sides of Ba Na Hills — two different worlds.

Ba Na Hills, the Golden Bridge and the French Village reveal another Da Nang — high above the clouds, among mountains and mist. Today people come here for the Golden Bridge, the streets of the French Village and the panoramas from the top. But once these mountains knew only the whisper of the wind and the steps of ancient tribes…

Legend has it that stone giants sleep beneath the hills. On full-moon nights they wake and beat underground drums to rouse the mountains and warm the cold mist. One day a giant raised his stone palms to the sky, and the Golden Bridge appeared between them. And where people first heard its heart beating, the French Village grew over time.

Whoever hears the giants' rumble among the clouds will leave a piece of their heart in these mountains.

The tour price includes: hotel transfer both ways · English- or Russian-speaking guide · a bottle of water · all arrangements

Entrance tickets include: round-trip cable car · Golden Bridge · French Village · Le Jardin D’Amour gardens · temple area · seasonal shows · free entertainment · buffet · craft beer voucher

Not included: Debay wine cellars · Alpine Coaster · museum and interactive zones · rock climbing · prize games — most extra activities cost about $4
