FAQ

There is no child rate on tours from Nha Trang — one price for everyone, adults and children alike. Separate child tickets by height exist only at Vinpearl.
Prices are per person. All tours include hotel transfer both ways and an English- or Russian-speaking guide. Lunch, entrance tickets, the boat and gear are included where stated in the tour description. Anything paid separately is also listed in the description.
Where there is a choice of format, we list both. Group — a trip with other travelers in a shared vehicle or boat. VIP — a separate departure or a different class: for Dalat it's a VIP limousine and drinks with lunch, on the Marmoris yacht the upper deck with a jet ski, on the Northern islands a speedboat for a group of up to 15 that leaves right away and reaches the islands first.
From your hotel in Nha Trang. If you are staying in Cam Ranh or coming from Da Nang, tell us a convenient place and we'll pick you up there.
For sea trips and tours of two days or more — yes. Send a photo of your passport and the page with the visa stamp in advance.
Tour prices are in US dollars. There are two ways to pay.

In two steps. A deposit of 50% or more — no later than one day before the tour. The balance is paid to the guide in cash before departure, in dong or dollars.

Fully online. 100% — also no later than one day before the tour.

Online we accept: rubles via SBP transfer, dong via QR code, USDT via Bybit UID transfer. Cash dollars — in some cases our staff member can come to you, only for hotels in the tourist area.

Cash dollars: only 100-dollar bills with the blue stripe, without creases, stamps or writing. Change is given in dong.

We do not accept: old-design dollars, bills smaller than 100 dollars, card payments via terminal, crypto wallets.

Rubles and dong are converted at the company's internal rate on the day of payment.
Programs in Nha Trang are group tours, priced per person. Some routes have a VIP format — a separate departure or a different service class. If you want a tour just for your own party — message us and we'll quote it.
